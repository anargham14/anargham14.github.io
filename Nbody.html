N-body Simulations in Cosmology

As a part of the course requirements for the Cosmology (PH4213) course, we had to present on a topic of choice. I, as a part of a team of 4 others, decided to overview how N-body simulations have been crucial to the development of physical cosmology as a precision science.

We briefly reviewed how the N-body equations are setup from the fluid equations, how these equations are solved on a computer at a given time instance, and how these solutions are then propagated over time. We concluded with a discussion on the various data products obtained from running these simulations, how they have directed cosmology research, and what is the way forward.

My personal contribution was elucidating how force calculations are performed in N-body simulations. I decided to cover aspects of P2P and PM methods of force calculation in detail, while also giving a brief overview of TreePM, and $P^3$M.

The primary references were Angulo and Hahn, "Large-scale dark matter simulations" (2021) and the classic book by Hockney and Eastwood (1984).
